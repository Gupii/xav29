Chassis & GPU Deep Cleaning.

Restoring original thermal efficiency and airflow.

Dust buildup inside dirty PC cases directly chokes fan blades, clogs heatsink fins, and causes severe thermal throttling under heavy gaming loads. We utilize high-pressure electric air dust blowers to blast away trapped dust bunnies from tight GPU shroud crevices, power supply shrouds, and intake filters.

After blowing out loose debris, all fan blades, PCB surfaces, and case interior walls are thoroughly wiped clean down to a shine using IPA 99% alcohol Why 99% Isopropyl Alcohol?
99% anhydrous IPA evaporates rapidly with zero conductive water residue, safely dissolving caked-on dust film, oils, and dried thermal paste without risking oxidation on delicate circuit boards. for a sterile, spotless finish.

In-House Service Location Disclaimer

For all computers requiring a deep clean, we perform the service strictly in-house at my place. The high-pressure dust blowers and specialized cleaning tools I use are non-portable, and handling heavy dust/dirt extraction is significantly safer and cleaner in a familiar workshop environment.
