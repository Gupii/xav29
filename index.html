Chassis & GPU Deep Cleaning.

Restoring original thermal efficiency and airflow.

Dust buildup directly chokes fan blades, clogs heatsink fins, and causes thermal throttling. We utilize high-pressure electric air blowers to blast away trapped debris from tight crevices, power supply shrouds, and intake filters.

After blowing out loose debris, all fan blades and surfaces are thoroughly wiped down using IPA 99% alcohol Why 99% Isopropyl Alcohol?
99% anhydrous IPA evaporates rapidly with zero conductive water residue, safely dissolving caked-on oils and dried thermal paste without risking oxidation. for a clean, sterile finish.

In-House Service Location Disclaimer

For all computers requiring a deep clean, service is performed strictly in-house at my workshop setup where high-pressure blowers and extraction equipment are configured safely.
